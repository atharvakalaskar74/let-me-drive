How Let Me Drive Works

Simple, transparent workflow for both car owners and professional drivers

For Car Owners:

1

Register & Add Car

Add your car specifications (transmission, fuel, model) for compatible driver matching.

2

Post Requirement

Set your dates, pickup location, destination, working hours, and offered rate.

3

AI Recommendations

View explainable match percentages and select from verified applicants.

4

Hire & Rate

Confirm booking with 1-click, enjoy a safe ride, and rate your driver afterwards.

For Professional Drivers:

1

Create Driver Profile

Showcase your license type, years of experience, and driving skills.

2

Browse Nearby Jobs

Search outstation, daily, hourly, or permanent jobs in your city.

3

Apply with Rate

Send custom application message and state your expected salary/rate.

4

Get Hired & Build Reputation

Complete assignments, earn direct payments, and accumulate 5-star reviews.

Why Choose Let Me Drive

Explainable AI Matching
